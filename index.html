Daybook ΓÇö Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Daybook is a screen-time and focus app for Windows. This policy explains exactly what it
records, where that goes, and what it never does.

It is short because there is not much to say. Daybook has no account, no server and no
telemetry. Nothing it records leaves your PC.

The short version
Data sent to us None. There is no server to send it to.
Accounts None. You never create one or sign in.
Telemetry, analytics, crash reporting None.
Advertising, tracking, profiling None. Your data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.
Third-party services None, except Microsoft's own Store service, which Daybook asks for your trial and purchase status, and which handles the purchase.
Where your data lives One folder on your PC, private to Daybook. Settings ΓåÆ Your data ΓåÆ Open data folder shows you exactly where.
Uninstalling Removes that folder and everything in it.

What Daybook records

To tell you where your time went, Daybook records the following on your own computer:

  • The application in the foreground ΓÇö its process name, its file path, and the name
    Windows reports for it (for example "Google Chrome").
  • The window title of that application.
  • The website host you are on, when the foreground window is a browser ΓÇö the host only
    (github.com), never the full address, never page contents, never form data.
  • When you were away ΓÇö periods with no keyboard or mouse input, so idle time is not
    counted as screen time. It records that you were idle, never what you typed.
  • What you create in the app ΓÇö focus sessions and the intentions you type for them, tasks,
    goals, limits, blocklists, schedules, projects with their clients and hourly rates, manual
    time entries, profiles, the categories and rules you set, and your settings.

That is the complete list.

What Daybook never records

  • Keystrokes. Daybook uses Windows' idle timer, which reports only how long ago the last
    input happened. It cannot see what you type and does not try.
  • Screenshots or screen contents.
  • Page contents, form fields, passwords, messages or documents.
  • Full web addresses. Only the host.
  • Audio. Daybook checks whether the microphone is in use, so it can stay quiet during a
    call. It never opens, records or listens to it.
  • Your location, contacts, files, camera, or anything on your network.

Where it is stored

Everything sits in a single SQLite database, a settings file and a log file, in one folder.
Daybook sees it as:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\Daybook

When Daybook is installed from the Microsoft Store, Windows keeps that folder in the app's
own private storage (under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages), so other programs do not see it at
that path. Settings ΓåÆ Your data ΓåÆ Open data folder always opens the real location.

It is not encrypted, because it never leaves your machine and is protected by your Windows
user account like the rest of your profile. Anyone with administrative access to your PC could
read it, exactly as they could read your documents.

What you can do with it

  • Export everything to CSV or JSON from Reports, at any time. Exports, timesheets and
    backups are written to Documents\Daybook, because they are your files rather than the
    app's own state. If your Documents folder is synced ΓÇö to OneDrive, for instance ΓÇö anything
    you export will sync with it. Backups are encrypted with your passphrase; CSV and JSON
    exports are not, so treat them the way you would any other document containing your history.
  • Delete everything without uninstalling: Settings ΓåÆ Your data ΓåÆ Delete all my data
    opens the folder; close Daybook and delete the folder, and nothing is left.
  • Uninstall ΓÇö Windows removes the app's own folder and everything in it. No residue, no
    server-side copy, because there was never a server-side copy. Files you exported or backed
    up to Documents are deliberately left alone: deleting things you chose to save is not
    something an uninstaller should do on your behalf.

Permissions Daybook asks Windows for

  • runFullTrust ΓÇö required to read which window is in the foreground. This is the
    capability the entire app is built on. Nothing broader is declared.
  • startupTask ΓÇö only if you switch on "Start Daybook when I sign in". It is off by
    default, and Windows gives you its own separate toggle for it in Settings ΓåÆ Apps ΓåÆ Startup.

Purchases

Daybook is a one-time purchase made entirely through the Microsoft Store, after a free
trial that the Store itself runs. When Daybook starts, it asks the Store whether this copy is in
its trial or has been bought - that answer is the only thing it receives, and nothing about your
activity is sent with the question. Microsoft handles the payment; Daybook never sees, receives
or stores your card, billing address or any other payment information. Microsoft's own privacy statement covers that transaction:
https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

Children

Daybook is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify anyone, of any
age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it, the new version
ships with the app update that makes the change, and the copy published online is updated at the
same time. Since Daybook has no server, there is no way
for the terms to change quietly underneath you.
